Segregation Standard

GCC Mandated 4-Bin Waste Model

Segregating waste at source ensures higher recycling efficiency and keeps toxic materials out of Chennai’s dumpsites.

🟢

Green Bin

Wet / Organic Waste

What Goes In:

  • Kitchen food scraps & leftovers
  • Fruit & vegetable peels
  • Eggshells, tea leaves & coffee grounds
  • Garden leaves & withered flowers

Strict No-Nos:

No plastic garbage liners! Empty wet waste directly into the bin.

Destination: Local Composting / Biogas Plants
🔵

Blue Bin

Dry / Recyclables

What Goes In:

  • Clean paper, cartons & newspapers
  • Rinsed milk packets & PET bottles
  • Rigid plastics, plastic containers
  • Glass bottles & clean metal cans

Strict No-Nos:

No food-soiled paper or oily containers. Rinse before discarding!

Destination: Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs) / Scrap Buyers
🔴

Red Bin

Sanitary Waste

What Goes In:

  • Used diapers & sanitary napkins
  • Used tissues, cotton & bandages
  • Expired medical dressings

Safety Rule:

Must be securely wrapped in paper marked with a red cross to protect sanitation workers.

Destination: GCC High-Temperature Incineration
⚫

Black Bin

Hazardous & Special Care

What Goes In:

  • Expired medicines & pill strips
  • Insecticide cans & chemical bottles
  • Batteries, CFLs & fluorescent tubes
  • Paint, varnish & solvent tins

Danger Warning:

Never throw into normal garbage! Toxic chemicals leak into groundwater.

Destination: Certified Hazardous Waste Recyclers
